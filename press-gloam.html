Description

Short: A co-op roguelite set in dark civil war era America. You and everything else in the land have been tainted rotten. Find redemption in vanquishing the embodiments of sin.

GLOAM
You, and all else alike, are corrupt with sin, stuck in this vile wasteland. The only path to atone is to vanquish the tarnished foes roaming about. Do so by exploring, killing monsters and trading blackrock and souls for items that create unique item combinations.

YOU ARE NOT ALONE
Corrupt as your allies may be, fighting with up to 3 teammates may prove favorable on your quest to redemption.

VILE CREATURES
The wasteland is swarming with tarnished wicked creatures. Vanquish enemy fodder, flee from foes stronger than you, challenge mini-bosses and eventually topple the amalgamations of sin, plaguing this land.

EXPLORATION
No two ventures into the wasteland are the same. Completely procedurally generated layouts and several uniquely purposed rooms. Choose how you explore the wasteland, where to allocate resources and what rooms to risk going into at the cost of getting stronger or potentially dying.

Features

  • Online co-op — play with your friends
  • Procedurally generated dungeons — a new layout every run
  • Skill-based combat — dodge, parry and fight
  • 100+ unique items — collect items that combine and uniquely synergize
  • 30+ vile creatures — crawl your way through the wasteland fighting unique creatures
  • Handmade — every asset is handpainted
